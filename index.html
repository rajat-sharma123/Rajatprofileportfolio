CardioGuard AI

Clinical Cardiovascular Risk Stratification & Predictive Decision Support

End-to-end clinical decision support platform designed to stratify cardiovascular disease risk using patient vitals, electrocardiographic markers, and treadmill exercise stress biomarkers across 5,000 clinical records.

✓
Clinical Recall Prioritization (89.64%): Benchmarked Random Forest, Logistic Regression, and Decision Tree, achieving 89.1% accuracy and 95.6% ROC-AUC while optimizing sensitivity to minimize fatal false negatives.
✓
Real-Time Streamlit Dashboard: Bedside interactive triage web app with continuous vital sliders, multi-model consensus prediction, live risk gauge, and guideline-based clinical prescriptions.
✓
Gini Feature Interpretability: Quantified diagnostic impact of 13 physiological features, isolating chest pain type (cp), maximum heart rate (thalach), and ST depression (oldpeak) as primary risk drivers.
